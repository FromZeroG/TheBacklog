    20 Minutes Till Dawn

    A bullet-heaven with real aim-your-own-gun ambition, held back from a clean positive by a stalled update cycle.

    Platform PC Genre Bullet Heaven Roguelike Released Jun 2023 (1.0) Logged 22 Hrs
    Official art · via Steam
    82% Mixed Bag

    20 Minutes Till Dawn departs from Vampire Survivors' template in one deliberate way: you aim. Every gun points where you point it, and that single change turns the genre's usual auto-fire kiting into something closer to a twin-stick shooter layered over roguelike upgrades. A roster of heroines each opens a different early build, and a Tomes system stacks passive modifiers on top of a pool of more than 50 weapon and item upgrades.

    Early runs feel great. The manual aiming rewards actual positioning rather than pure movement, and a cross-run rune system carries meaningful meta-progression forward, giving each unlocked rune a reason to chase the next run instead of repeating the last one.

    That strength thins out by the back half of a run. Destructoid's preview and Softpedia's review both flag the same problem: builds converge on the same handful of dominant weapon-and-tome combinations once a run runs long enough, flattening the choices that felt open early on. Screen readability breaks down at high enemy density, projectiles and effects piling into a wall that's hard to read at a glance, and most of what the game has to unlock shows up within the first two runs, leaving long-term progression thin for a genre built on repeat sessions.

    The real story is the gap between the game's reputation and its current state. Steam Community threads describe it outright as abandonware, and the developer's own Steam page currently points to a second, unrelated title as their active focus. That shows up directly in the numbers: All-Time reviews sit at 90 percent Very Positive, but the Recent bucket has dropped to 47 percent Mixed across 115 reviews, a split that tracks with the stalled update cycle rather than any change in the base game. OpenCritic lists a 77 average that undersells the split further, since only half the tracked critics gave it an outright recommend, and Metacritic carries no real Metascore, only two tracked reviews.

    Mixed bag, leaning positive. The manual aiming and early-run build variety earn the price on their own, but the update silence and the current-review slide are real enough to keep this out of a clean recommendation.
