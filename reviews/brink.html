    BRINK

    A parkour shooter with real mechanical ideas, undone by broken bot AI and a multiplayer population that's basically gone.

    Platform PC Genre Class-Based FPS Released May 2011 Logged 14 Hrs
    Official art · via Steam
    64% Mixed Bag

    BRINK builds its whole shooter around SMART, a parkour system that lets you vault, slide, and climb through a match without breaking stride or touching a jump button twice. Splash Damage built the class and mission systems around teamwork instead of a kill-death scoreboard: no lone-wolf frag counter, only an objective the whole team pushes toward one stage at a time. A full lobby turns the SMART system and the objective focus into something rare for a shooter of its era, chaotic in the way a coordinated push should be.

    Character and weapon customization back up the class system with real depth, praised across most reviews as the game's best-preserved feature, and the body-type and loadout options still give each class a distinct feel in a full lobby.

    The single-player campaign gives away the game's real weak point: it's the multiplayer wearing a story's clothes, six or seven cutscenes bolted onto matches against bots. Those bots are the bigger problem. Reviewers at launch called the AI "godawful" and "utterly atrocious," teammates who stand still and get gunned down instead of taking cover. BRINK also shipped without basic online infrastructure: no lobby system, no way to party up with friends without joining a match already in progress. Frame drops, texture pop-in, and audio desync rounded out a launch critics remember as rough even where the mechanics underneath worked.

    The bigger problem by 2026 is the population. Concurrent players sit around a dozen today, down from a recent peak of a little over fifty two years ago, and Steam reviews left in the last year describe servers standing empty more often than not. Steam's own review data reflects the split verdict: Mixed at roughly 60 percent, matching Metacritic's 70 from launch-era critics who liked the ideas more than the execution.

    Worth a download since it's free, but go in knowing the ideas outshine the execution, and a full server isn't guaranteed.
