    The System

    How The Hell Does This Work?

    Forget scores out of ten. Nobody's agreed on what a 7 actually means since about 2004, and "8.5, would recommend" tells you nothing about whether the damn game did its job.

    Here, every review gets stamped with one number: how much of what the game promised you — in the trailer, the marketing, the hype train, all of it — actually showed up. Set your expectations at 100%. Anything under that, the game owed you something and skipped town. Anything over, it went further than it had to, and it earns the right to say so.

    <60% Whiffed It

    Broke promises, broke on launch, or just wasn't there. Wait for a sale, or don't bother.

    60–84% Mixed Bag

    Real highs, real lows. Worth it for the right person, not a slam dunk for everyone.

    85–109% Delivered

    Did what it said on the box. In this economy, that alone deserves applause.

    110%+ Exceeded
    Expectations

    The rare one that breaks its own scale. Doesn't happen often. You'll know when it does.

    About The Backlog

    One Person. Too Many Unfinished Games.

    The Backlog is one reviewer working through the pile everybody's got — the games bought on a whim, the ones friends swore were life-changing, the ones still shrink-wrapped out of guilt. Every review here gets played to a real ending, not a fifteen-minute impression.

    No review copies dictate the verdict, no score gets softened for a publisher relationship, and no paragraph gets padded with corporate press-speak. Just an honest read on whether the thing in front of you kept its word.
